Fees tenants have paid (admin, late, NSF, RBP, legal ...) that you post to the property as a bill. Post a fee only when it says OK to post, meaning none of that tenant's payments are still waiting to settle. Staff only.

How it works: Rentvine does not say which payment paid which fee, so a fee is held while that lease has any payment that is applied but not settled. It can hold a fee a little longer than needed, but never releases one early. "OK to post" hides fees where a bill on the same property and account already covers the amount the tenant paid.
